1. Get ready

  1. Use Chrome on desktop, v139+.
  2. Enable chrome://flags/#prompt-api-for-gemini-nano-multimodal-input, then restart Chrome.
  3. Click “Prepare on-device model” below. This calls initializeDeviceModel() — it must run after a click, before any practice turn.

If Nano is not available, practice still works: it falls back to the cloud model automatically.

2. Pick a scenario

Your goal: speak in English for 4–6 turns. The tutor replies in simple English, corrects you gently, and keeps the conversation going.
